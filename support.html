How to play

Drag the left page over the spine to fold it. Red ink on the left page lands on the right page, mirrored. Tap the page to roll the ball. Reach the star, catch every ink drop, and use no more folds than par for three stars. Tap How to play on the home screen for the tour and practice pages.

A friend's link won't open

Make sure Crease is installed, then tap the link again. If it opens a web page, tap Open in Crease.

Restoring the full notebook

Open a locked world and tap Restore on the unlock card. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with.

Reminders and widgets

Crease can remind you when a new Daily Fold is ready and before your streak runs out. Turn reminders on or off in My notebook, on the Stats tab. To add a widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget and pick Crease.

iPhone Duo

Close the phone a little and the page folds with it. Open it flat to unfold.

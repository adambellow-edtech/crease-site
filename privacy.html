Privacy Policy

Last updated November 3, 2026.

Crease doesn't collect, sell or share personal data. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts of our own.

What stays on your iPhone

Your stars, times, streak, stickers, settings and the pages you build are saved on your device only.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your scores go to Apple's leaderboards and your Game Center name can appear on challenge links you choose to send. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center.

Pages you publish

If you choose to publish a page you built, the page itself (its layout and name) is stored in Apple's iCloud so other players can play it. Nothing else about you is attached.

Links you share

A shared link holds the page, and for a race your time, fold count and fold timings. It's only sent to whoever you send it to.

Purchases

The full notebook is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details.

Children

Crease doesn't knowingly collect any information from children.

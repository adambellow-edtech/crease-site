Fold to solve

Ink on the left page moves when you fold it over. Drop a ledge into place, flatten a pencil wall, or carry the ball across the spine.

Timing matters: fold too early and you block the way, too late and the ball falls.

Three worlds, sixty pages

Each world brings new pieces, with a quick demo the first time you meet one.

Race your friends

Clear a page and send it to a friend. They tap the link and race a see-through ghost of your run.

A new Daily Fold every day, the same page for everyone. Keep your streak and share your result.

Build

Draw ledges, drop in springs, fans, stains and holes, set the star and send your page as a link.

Collect

Stars unlock new paper (Blueprint, Neon, Chalkboard) and new balls. Stickers fill your notebook's cover.

Learn as you go

Pick your paper

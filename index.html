Crease

Fold the page. Roll the ball. Reach the star.

A puzzle game in a notebook. Fold the left page over and its ink lands on the right, just in time to catch the ball.

Get it on the App Store See how it plays

Fold to solve

Ink on the left page moves when you fold it over. Drop a ledge into place, flatten a pencil wall, or carry the ball across the spine.

Timing is everything: fold too early and you block the way, too late and the ball falls.

Five worlds, 100 pages

Every world brings new pieces to fold, with a quick demo the first time you meet one. The pages get trickier as you go.

Springs Paper fans Ink stains Hole punches Scissors Tape Magnet clips Erasers

Daily Fold

A new page every day, the same one for everyone. Keep your streak going and share your result.

This Week

Five pages built around one piece, played under Hard Mode rules. Race everyone on the weekly leaderboard.

Hard Mode

Clear a world and its Hard Mode opens: every ink drop, par folds and a clock. Every page also hides a gold seal.

Race your friends

Clear a page and send it to a friend. They tap the link and race a see-through ghost of your run.

Win, and the confetti flies.

37 trophies to win

Finish worlds, keep streaks, earn gold seals and beat Hard Mode. Every trophy lands on your notebook's cover and in Game Center.

Stars unlock new paper and new balls along the way.

Build your own

Draw ledges, drop in springs, fans, scissors and magnets, set the star, then beat your own page to share it as a link.

Pick your paper

Origami, Sketchbook, Blueprint, Neon or Chalkboard. Every page gets its own scene.

On your Home Screen

Widgets show today's Daily Fold, This Week's five pages and your streak. Gentle reminders keep it going, and you can turn them off any time.

Made for iPhone Duo

Close your Duo a little and the page folds with it.
On any other iPhone, drag the page over.
